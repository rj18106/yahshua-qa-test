## Bug 002 (The other years are showing inside the year filter in Payroll Calculator)

## Description
The other years inside the year filter remains visible even when a selected year contains no data records.

## Severity
Low

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Access the PH Payroll Calculator
2. Access the Payroll Calculator screen by clicking the Calculator in the navbar
3. Click the Year filter

## Expected Result
Years that has no existing data should not display inside the year filter

## Actual Result
Other years such as 2024, 2028, and 2029 is showing in the list inside the year filter
