## Bug 003 (Year Filter: Incorrect Years Displated in Dropdown)

## Description
The Year filter inside the Payroll History screen is displaying incorrect year options.
- Year 2027 is not displayed in the dropdown even though payroll records exist for that year
- Year 2022, and 2023 are displayed in the dropdown even though there are no payroll records for those years

## Severity
Low

## Preconditions
- Payroll records for year 2027 should exist

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Access the PH Payroll Calculator
2. Access the Payroll History screen by clicking the Payroll History in the navbar
3. Click the "Year" filter
4. Check the list inside the filter
5. Select year 2023
6. Check the records in the table
7. Select year 2022
8. Check the records in the tabl

## Expected Result
- The year filter dropdown should only display years that have existing payroll records
- Year 2027 should include in the dropdown since there are records for that year
- Year 2022, and 2023 should not be displayed in the dropdown since there are no existing payroll records for those years

## Actual Result
- Year 2027 is not displayed in the Year filter dropdown even though payroll records exist
- Year 2022, and 2023 are displayed in the dropdown even though there are no payroll records for those years
